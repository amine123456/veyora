Use Veyora only for lawful, authorized activity that respects people, systems, and network resources.

Prohibited activity

You may not use the service for unlawful activity; unauthorized access, scanning, exploitation, malware, botnets, or denial-of-service activity; phishing, impersonation, fraud, spam, or deceptive traffic; harassment, threats, exploitation, or distribution of illegal content; infringement of privacy, intellectual property, or other rights; bypassing security controls, access restrictions, or platform rules without authorization; manipulation of advertising, engagement, marketplaces, ticketing, or financial systems; or activity that degrades the service or exposes infrastructure or network participants to unreasonable risk.

Your responsibilities

Obtain required authorization, follow applicable laws and destination terms, secure your credentials, limit collection to data you are entitled to process, and honor requests from affected parties. Do not conceal prohibited activity behind Veyora.

Enforcement

We may investigate credible reports, preserve relevant records, rate-limit traffic, suspend credentials, or close accounts. Severe or repeated violations may result in immediate termination without refund where permitted by law. We may cooperate with lawful requests from authorities.

Report abuse

Use the support form and choose “Report abuse.” Include timestamps, target hostnames or IPs, request details, and any Veyora-related evidence you have. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal data.
