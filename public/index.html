Questions, answered.

Are the IPs ethically sourced?

Yes. Every residential IP comes from a user who opted in, is paid for sharing bandwidth and can leave at any time. We audit our partners and enforce an acceptable use policy on all traffic.

Which countries can I target?

Any of 195. Add a country flag to your username, for example USER-country-de, or leave it off to use the whole pool. Every plan includes it.

Do GB expire?

Not inside your billing term. Monthly plans roll unused traffic over for 30 days, and annual plans keep it for the full 12 months.

Which protocols do you support?

HTTP, HTTPS and SOCKS5 on every plan. Authenticate with a username and password, or by whitelisting your server IPs.

Do you offer a trial?

We offer a 3-day refund instead of a trial. Start on Starter, test on your real targets and get your money back if it is not a fit.

Do you support unlimited threads?

Yes. We never cap concurrent connections or threads. Only traffic is metered, so you can scale your scrapers freely.

What is your refund policy?

Ask for a full refund within 3 days of your first purchase if you have used less than 2 GB. Contact support and we will process it.

Can I whitelist IPs?

Yes. Add up to 20 IPs per user on Starter, and unlimited IPs on Pro and Enterprise, from the dashboard or the API.

Ready for traffic that never gets blocked?

Start with 8 GB and add more when you need it. Your first 3 days are covered by our refund.
