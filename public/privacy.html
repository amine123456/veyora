Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Veyora collects, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We collect account information you provide, including your email address and securely hashed authentication data; purchase and transaction references needed to credit your account; proxy usage totals, connection metadata, and security logs needed to operate and protect the service; and information you submit through our support form.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, deliver purchased traffic, process and reconcile payments, provide support, prevent abuse and fraud, diagnose reliability issues, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our terms. We do not sell personal information.

Payments and service providers

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. They handle payment information under their own privacy terms; Veyora receives transaction status and identifiers needed to fulfill purchases. We may use infrastructure and security providers that process limited information on our behalf.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for service, security, accounting, dispute, and legal purposes. No system can guarantee absolute security, but we use access controls, transport encryption, and restricted credential storage designed to protect your data.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with your account, subject to security verification and lawful retention requirements. Submit a request through support. You can also stop using the service and avoid submitting optional information.

Cookies

We use an essential, secure session cookie to keep signed-in accounts authenticated. We do not currently use advertising cookies on this site.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, use our support form.
